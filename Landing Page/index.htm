The Bottleneck We Are Solving

Manufacturing is fast, but quality control is stuck in the past. We are eliminating these manual failure points:

Unstructured Data

Workers waste hours manually cross-referencing paper job cards, SOPs, and complex CAD drawings to find what to measure.

Delayed Error Detection

Manual data entry means mistakes are caught *after* the batch is made, leading to expensive rework and material waste.

The Knowledge Gap

Crucial inspection intuition lives in the heads of senior staff, making new-hire onboarding slow and prone to errors.

Under the Hood: What the AI Actually Does

How our models transform unstructured manufacturing documents into active workflows.

1. Vision AI: Drawing Parsing

The Tech: Our computer vision model ingests 2D engineering drawings and automatically extracts critical dimensions, tolerances, and part features.

The Fix: No more squinting at blueprints. The system instantly knows exactly what needs to be measured.

2. NLP: Smart SOP Digitization

The Tech: Natural Language Processing reads static Job Cards and SOPs, breaking them down into a step-by-step, logic-driven digital checklist.

The Fix: Standardizes the inspection process so any new EDGE worker can perform like a 10-year veteran.

3. Real-Time Anomaly Detection

The Tech: As workers input measurements, the system instantly validates the data against the extracted tolerances from the Vision model.

The Fix: Flags out-of-spec parts instantly on the floor, stopping defective production runs dead in their tracks.

4. Generative Reporting

The Tech: Synthesizes the parsed requirements and the worker's input data to auto-populate compliance documents.

The Fix: Eliminates manual admin work. Traceability records are generated perfectly and instantly for every single part.
